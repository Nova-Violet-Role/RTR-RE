## What v3 is

pairs with no v1/v2 label (`v3_gap.py`). v3 grades exactly those 12
against the TRUE v2 intent strings and frozen v2 precedents — 10

`45b76e1624661c1` after the build (byte-identical, no label moved).

validation errors empty. Files: `eval/graded/queries-v3.json`,
`eval/graded/qrels-v3.json`.
